# Doubles pairing and main-squad eligibility

Approved by the user on 5 November 2026.

1. An administrator creates a doubles category and manages the tournament's main-squad roster.
2. A player invites an existing account by its exact institutional email. Players cannot select or edit squad status.
3. A main-squad player may partner with a non-main-squad player. Two main-squad players cannot pair. Two non-main-squad players may pair.
4. The recipient accepts or declines; the sender may cancel while pending. Both identities must be available in that category. An invitation alone does not register anyone or reserve capacity.
5. Acceptance checks the current roster, category state and duplicate membership under a category lock. A valid pair receives one confirmed place or one waitlist position.
6. Either partner can withdraw their entire pair while registration is open. Replacing a partner requires a new invitation and consent. Pair membership is immutable after registration closes.
7. Group allocation, seeds, standings, match sides and knockout winners use the inviter as the entry's representative. Both names are displayed. Four-athlete scheduling and individual ranking publication include the partner.
8. Squad edits must not create an ineligible active pair. The roster freezes once any category has left the draft/open registration stages.

This amendment authorizes doubles pairing and integration with the existing tournament flow. It does not authorize QR check-in or automated doubles service rotation.
